## Acceptance conditions

- A quota reserve or a 429 leaves a durable `waiting_quota` checkpoint with an exact retry target.
- A timeout/provider outage leaves a durable `waiting_provider` checkpoint and resumes from the same chunk.
- Global provider waits do not increment scripture retry attempts.
- A retry that becomes due during a long run is reinserted before lower-priority new work.
- Board and generated reports agree on complete translation, tags, retries and blocked work.
- Closing the board, sleeping, rebooting, or reaching quota never loses the next action needed for handoff.
